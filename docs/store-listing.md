## App name

Norma Camera

## Short description (≤80 chars)

Point your camera — Norma shows you when the frame sings, and why.

## Full description

Norma Camera is a viewfinder with taste.

Choose a canon — Thirds, Phi, Root 2, Diagonals — and the frame shows its
construction: faint guide lines, marked power points. When the visual mass of
your subject settles onto a point, it lights up, the resonance arc fills, and
the phone gives a small tick. Tap WHY? at any moment and Norma explains the
score: which guide, how far, what contributed.

Norma is openly subjective. These canons are editorial choices — classical
opinions from painting and photography, openly held, not objective truth, and
the app says so on its own screens.

Honest by construction:

- All analysis happens on your device: a coarse luminance grid, nothing else.
- No face detection, no object recognition, no AI model, no cloud.
- No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no network use in the app's own code.
- Works without Google Play Services (GrapheneOS-friendly).
- Auto-capture exists but is strictly opt-in, behind an ARM switch with
  conservative wording about what it can actually know.

Shoot, review with the construction drawn over your photo, share clean or
with the canon burned in, or hand the canon context to Norma Construct on
norma.science to keep working on the composition.
