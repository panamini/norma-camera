# Norma Camera — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-10

Norma Camera is a composition viewfinder. It is built so that your data
cannot leave your device, because the app has nowhere to send it.

## What the app does with the camera

The camera feed is analyzed on your device only: a coarse 32×24 luminance
grid computed from the live frame drives exposure, sharpness, contrast-mass,
and line readouts. No frame, no pixel data, and no derived analysis ever
leaves the device. There is no face detection, no object recognition, no
machine-learning model, and no cloud processing of any kind.

## What the app stores

Photos you capture are written to the app's local storage on your device.
The session gallery lives in memory and empties when the app closes. If you
use "+ CANON", a downscaled copy with the guide drawing is written to the
app's cache on your device.

## What leaves the device

Nothing, unless you explicitly send it:

- **Share**: hands a photo to the Android share sheet; what happens next is
  between you and the app you pick.
- **Construct**: opens norma.science in your browser with the canon name,
  score, label, and capture time in the URL — never the photo, never a file
  path, never an identifier.

## What the app collects

Nothing. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no
accounts, no network calls in the app's own code. The app does not request
network permission for its features and does not depend on Google Play
Services.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: required to show the viewfinder and take photos. That is the
  only permission the app requests.
