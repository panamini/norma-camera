# Norma Camera — Release Notes

## 0.2.0-beta.1 (2026-10-10) — Harmony Viewfinder

The V1 detection engine (complete and field-validated in June) gets its
product: the harmony viewfinder.

- **Canons**: Thirds · Phi · Root 2 · Diagonals as named, switchable lenses —
  guide geometry plus marked power points, with honest editorial descriptions.
- **Lit nodes**: power points glow as the subject's visual mass approaches;
  a light haptic tick on lock.
- **Resonance arc**: the composition score fills a ring around the shutter.
- **WHY? sheet**: one tap explains the score — nearest guide, distance,
  line-signal contribution, what the evidence actually is, and the canon's
  own editorial note.
- **Capture flow**: session gallery, review screen with the construction
  re-projected over the photo, system share, "+ CANON" burn-in share
  (on-device, downscaled copy), and the Norma Construct hand-off.
- **Modes**: MANUAL tap and AUTO (native visual mass with honest fallback)
  up front; placeholder/simulated detectors and debug overlays behind `···`.
- **Unchanged**: detection algorithms, scoring formula and thresholds,
  readiness gating, auto-capture behavior (still strictly opt-in), 4 fps
  latest-frame-only analysis, no-cloud/no-ML guarantees.

New Expo-native dependencies (dev-client rebuild required): `expo-haptics`,
`expo-sharing`, `expo-file-system`.
