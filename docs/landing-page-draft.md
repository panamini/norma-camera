# norma.science/camera — Landing Page Draft + Video Script (S3)

To implement in the norma-square repo (paper/graphite design language, mono
micro-labels, same tokens as the app). This doc is the content contract.

## Page structure

### 1. Hero
- H1: **Point your camera — Norma shows you when the frame sings, and why.**
- Sub: *A viewfinder with taste. Classical canons, openly held. Everything on
  your device — nothing ever leaves it.*
- Media: the 30 s lock video (muted autoplay, replay on tap).
- CTA: `JOIN THE BETA (FREE)` → Play internal/open track link.
  Post-launch: `GET NORMA CAMERA · 9,99 €` + `F-DROID (FREE)`.

### 2. The moment (3 frames, side by side)
1. Faint canon lines, marked power points — *Choose a canon: Thirds · Phi ·
   Root 2 · Diagonals.*
2. Node glowing, arc filling — *Your subject settles on a power point. It
   lights up. The arc fills. A tick in your hand.*
3. WHY? sheet — *Tap WHY? and Norma explains the score. No black box.*

### 3. Honest by construction (the moat, stated plainly)
- No face detection. No object recognition. No AI model. No cloud.
- No account, no analytics, no ads, no network use at all.
- Works without Google Play Services — GrapheneOS-friendly.
- "Norma's canons are editorial choices — classical opinions, openly held.
  Not objective truth." (the in-app sentence, quoted verbatim)

### 4. The Harmony Journal
- *Your eye, progressing.* Streak, seven-day average, best frame per canon —
  kept on your device only.

### 5. The Norma Card
- Show one card full-width. *Share any frame as a print: its construction,
  its canon, its resonance.*

### 6. Construct bridge
- *Keep working on the composition:* camera hands the canon context to Norma
  Construct. One line + link to /app.

### 7. FAQ (short)
- Why is there no iOS version? (Android first; iOS later.)
- Why does it cost money? (No ads, no data — you are the customer, not the
  product. One purchase, for life.)
- What does "no recognition" mean exactly? (32×24 luminance grid, contrast
  mass + line geometry; link to the privacy policy.)
